My method rests on three pillars:

✔

Healthy, Balanced Nutrition

No starving, no harsh restrictions, no living by calorie counts. I teach the core working principles of healthy eating - setting your daily routine and meal schedule, and building your own filling, psychologically comfortable diet that fits your goal.

✔

Psychology of Eating Behavior

We work with the causes of overeating, not just its consequences. I teach you to better "listen" to your body's signals - recognizing genuine physical hunger in time and understanding what to do about emotional or external triggers.

✔

The Right Amount of Physical Activity

I choose physical activity individually - the kind that gives you energy instead of draining you emotionally. Exercise isn't punishment, it's CARE for yourself.

A healthy body and a peaceful mind are the foundation of a quality life!
That is exactly the priority I bring to our work together.

My nutrition plans are built from ordinary foods available in any supermarket - no "exotic" ingredients. Budget preferences can also be taken into account.

Your daily water intake is calculated individually - based on your actual body weight, your physical activity level, and how much water you're already drinking right now. From these numbers, we work out a realistic, comfortable daily water target just for you.

My work with you would focus not on restrictions, but on stabilizing your nervous system "through the plate." We'll set up an eating routine that removes physical hunger, and choose psychological tools for working with your emotions. Working on nutrition during stress isn't about restriction - it's about care and a resource for your mind.

During the active phase, the most comfortable and informative period for nutrition check-ins is every 14-21 days. That's the optimal window to track progress, work through any difficulties, and adjust the plan.

For daily support and quick feedback on your "plate" and emotional state, I also offer a "photo report" format.

During the maintenance phase, once results have stabilized, it's 1 check-in per month.

Psychology sessions happen once a week or every 2 weeks, depending on how much support you need in the process.

I build an INDIVIDUAL nutrition plan for each person - taking into account your unique physiology, food preferences, existing eating habits, daily schedule, physical activity, and psychological traits. I adapt it completely to you, so our process together is comfortable and aligned with your goal.

I don't put together a plan "by eye," use templates, or just hand you a list of foods - I help you understand why you eat the way you do, and how to change those patterns in a way that's psychologically comfortable for you.
