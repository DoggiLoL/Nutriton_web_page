My method rests on three pillars:

Healthy, Balanced Nutrition

No crash diets, no harsh restrictions, no calorie counting. I teach you the core principles of healthy eating: setting up your daily routine and meal schedule, and building a filling, psychologically comfortable way of eating that fits your goal.

The Psychology of Eating Behavior

We work on the causes of overeating, not just its effects. I help you tune in to your body's signals, recognize true physical hunger in time, and know what to do when emotions or outside triggers take over.

The Right Amount of Movement

I tailor physical activity to you, so it gives you energy instead of draining you. Exercise isn't punishment. It's a way to take care of yourself.

We bring all three together in one personalized plan, step by step, at a pace that feels comfortable to you.

A healthy body and a peaceful mind are the foundation of a good quality of life.
That's the priority I bring to our work together.

Psychology

Initial Session (Assessment):
  • Assessment of your psycho-emotional state (completing a personalized Client Questionnaire and relevant diagnostic tools)
  • Defining session goals and setting specific objectives
  • Identifying limiting beliefs and unhelpful behavior patterns
  • Analyzing your thought patterns (how your thoughts affect your emotions and actions)
  • Uncovering automatic thoughts that may be holding you back
  • Psychoeducation: understanding how your mind works in your specific situation
  • Building a plan for our work together and setting homework
Follow-Up Sessions:
  • Reviewing progress: going over diagnostic results and discussing your homework
  • Tracking behavioral changes: looking at how your reactions to triggers have shifted
  • Working with cognitive distortions: replacing unhelpful thoughts with more balanced ones
  • Behavioral strategies: introducing and testing new behavior patterns so changes stick
  • Adjusting the focus of our work as your needs and progress evolve

Session frequency: once every 7-14 days, determined individually based on your goals, initial assessment results, and needs.
